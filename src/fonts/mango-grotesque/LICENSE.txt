Consequences for this repository
--------------------------------
- The .woff files in this folder are the files exactly as supplied. They must
  never be subsetted, re-encoded, converted to WOFF2, renamed internally or
  edited in any way. Only the *file names* have been changed.
- Because they cannot be subsetted, Mango Grotesque is reserved for display
  sizes (t-display-l and larger) and counters only. Everything smaller uses
  Archivo.
- Mango Grotesque lacks the Hausa hooked letters (Ɓ ɓ Ɗ ɗ Ƙ ƙ Ƴ ƴ), the naira
  sign (₦) and arrow/star glyphs. Those fall back to Archivo; arrows and stars
  are drawn as SVG components instead of characters.
