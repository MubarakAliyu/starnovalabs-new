01 · About us

The Company Behind the Work

StarNova Labs Ltd is a Nigerian technology company operating at the intersection of education and technology.

We design and deliver technology learning programmes for children and young people, and we build the digital products that support and extend that learning beyond the classroom.

Our flagship programme, Kids in Tech, has delivered multiple bootcamp cohorts teaching Scratch programming, web development and robotics through practical, project-based instruction.

Alongside these programmes we are developing a product portfolio — led by KITOS — intended to move learning from periodic bootcamps to continuous, accessible digital education.

What began as a series of bootcamps has grown into a company with a defined team, a documented curriculum, and a product pipeline built to scale that impact well beyond a single classroom.

03 · Leadership message

Message from the Founder & CEO

Founder portraitAliyu Mubarak
Founder & CEO

Aliyu Mubarak

Company direction, strategy, product and curriculum design.

“Understanding matters more than finishing the syllabus.”

When we started, the goal was simple: put real technology in the hands of children early enough to change how they see it. Not a lecture about computers — a project they build themselves.

Four cohorts later, that conviction has held. We have learned that a child who understands one concept deeply will go further than one who has been rushed through ten. It is why our instructors are told plainly that completing the curriculum is never more important than a student actually understanding it.

We have also grown as a company. What was once three founders teaching every class is now a team of educators, coordinators and technologists. We are formalising our structure, documenting our curriculum, and building KITOS so that learning does not stop when a bootcamp ends.

Our ambition is straightforward: to build a technology company whose educational products reach students far beyond the rooms we can physically fill — properly documented, properly staffed, and financially able to invest in its own growth.

Aliyu Mubarak
Founder & Chief Executive Officer

05 · Flagship programme

Kids in Tech

Practical technology skills for children, delivered through intensive, project-based bootcamps.

Kids in Tech is StarNova Labs' flagship education programme. Students learn by building — games, websites, working robots — rather than by listening. Each cohort runs across multiple learning tracks so children can find the discipline that fits them.

Returning students progress rather than repeat: those who have attended before are grouped separately and given more advanced projects, so every cohort moves them forward.

06 · Curriculum

Our Learning Tracks

Three tracks, each with its own progression. Students begin with the foundation and specialise from there.

Track01

Scratch Programming

Foundation · Logic & thinking

Computational thinking, programming logic, sprites, events and interactions. Students build games and animated stories, learning to structure ideas before syntax.

Track02

Web Development

Structure · Building for the web

Practical, project-based instruction in web technologies. Students move from structure and styling into interaction, building real pages they can publish and show.

Track03

Robotics & Embedded Systems

Integration · Hardware & control

Sensors, pin mapping, wiring and physical computing — progressing to soldering and working demonstrations. Students learn to modify code deliberately, not copy it.

07 · Methodology

How We Teach

Five principles shape every class we run.

01

Practical before theoretical

Students build real projects from the first sessions. Concepts are introduced through what they are making, not ahead of it.

02

Differentiated instruction

Learners are grouped by level within the same class, so returning students advance while beginners build foundations — nobody repeats and nobody falls behind.

03

Understanding as the measure

Curriculum coverage is never the success metric. Instructors are explicitly directed to prioritise comprehension over completion.

04

Peer support structures

Students who grasp concepts quickly are seated with those who need help — building collaboration and lifting task completion across the class.

05

Continuity beyond the classroom

In-class tasks and take-home assignments reinforce learning, with KITOS extending it between cohorts.

“A child who understands one concept deeply will go further than one rushed through ten.”

The principle that governs how every StarNova Labs class is delivered.

12 · Market & partnerships

The Problem We Solve

Children grow up surrounded by technology with little opportunity to learn how it works — or how to build with it.

Access to practical, hands-on technology education remains limited, particularly outside major urban centres. Schools rarely have the capability in-house, and generic computer training too often stops at using software rather than creating it.

StarNova Labs exists to close that gap — giving children direct experience of programming, web development and robotics early enough to shape how they engage with technology for the rest of their lives.

13 · Future direction

Where We're Heading

Three structural shifts define the next phase of the company.

Shift 01

From programmes to products

Bootcamps generate revenue in cycles and depend on founder time. KITOS and the wider pipeline move the company toward products that generate revenue continuously and scale beyond direct delivery.

Shift 02

From founder-led to team-delivered

Bootcamp 4.0 was the first cohort delivered substantially by people other than the founders. Formalising roles and documenting responsibilities builds an organisation that operates without the founders teaching every class.

Shift 03

From informal to investable

Registration, compliance, documentation and clean reporting are the foundation for credible investor conversations and institutional partnerships.
